Adding Videos & Blog Posts

Guide for Alan

Your website has a built-in “control room” called the Studio. It’s where you add new videos, write blog posts, and update content. This guide walks you through it one step at a time.

Open the Studio here www.alanxai.com/studio

Sign in with your usual details. It’s worth bookmarking this page so it’s easy to find next time.

The golden rule: Draft vs Published

Nothing you type appears on the live website until you press Publish. Until then, your work is saved privately as a Draft that only you can see. So feel free to experiment - the live site stays exactly as it is until you decide it’s ready. When you’re happy, press the blue Publish button at the bottom-right.

How the screen is laid out

When you open the Studio you’ll see three columns, left to right:

  • Left (“Content”) - the main areas of your site: Portfolio, Blog, Services, and so on. Pick one to start.
  • Middle - the list of items inside whatever you picked (all your videos, or all your blog posts).
  • Right - the form where you actually type and edit. This is where the work happens.
Spotting what’s live: next to each item is a small dot. A green dot means it’s live on the site. A grey dot means it’s a draft, or has changes you haven’t published yet.

1Add a new video

Your showreels and films live in the Portfolio area.

1
In the left “Content” column, click Portfolio.
2
At the top of the middle list, click the + button.
A fresh, blank form opens on the right.
3
Title - type a name for the video.
e.g. “ALANXAI - AI Showreel 2026”. This is the name people see on the site.
4
Slug - click Generate.
This makes the web-address version of the title for you. You don’t need to change it.
5
Category - open the dropdown and choose the best fit.
e.g. AI Video, Short Film, Aerial, Animation.
6
Add the video. Easiest way: paste your Vimeo link into the Video URL box.
  • A YouTube link, or a direct MP4 link, works in the same box too.
  • The Vimeo URL box just above also works - either one is fine, you don’t need both.
  • You only need Video File Upload if the video isn’t online anywhere.
7
Thumbnail - the still image shown before the video plays.
Click Select or Upload and choose a nice frame. Optional, but it looks much better with one.
8
Description - a short line shown when someone hovers over the video.
Keep it brief - a sentence is plenty.
9
Year - type the year, e.g. 2026.
10
Press Publish (bottom-right). That’s it - it’s now on your site.
In short: if you can watch the video online, just paste its link. That’s by far the simplest route.

2Write a blog post

Blog posts live in the Blog area.

1
In the left column, click Blog, then Blog Posts.
2
Click the + at the top of the posts list.
A fresh, blank post opens on the right.
3
Title - the headline of your post.
4
Slug - click Generate (same as before; it builds the web address for you).
5
Published At - the date shown on the post.
It fills in today’s date automatically - leave it unless you want a different date.
6
Excerpt - a short preview, a sentence or two.
This is what people read in the blog list before they click in.
7
Main Image - the big photo at the top of the post.
Click Select or Upload. (Optional: Image Link URL lets that image link somewhere, e.g. a Vimeo video. Leave blank if not needed.)
8
Primary Category - pick the main topic.
e.g. AI, Film, Aerial. Click the field and choose one.
9
Categories - optional extra tags if the post fits more than one topic.
Click + Add item and pick from the list.
10
Body - the main article. Type away.
The little toolbar above the text lets you:
  • make text bold (B) or italic (i)
  • add bullet or numbered lists
  • add a link (the chain icon)
  • drop in an Image
  • embed a YouTube video
11
When you’re happy, press Publish.
Your work saves itself as a draft. Close the tab and come back any time - you’ll find it waiting in the Blog Posts list with a grey dot.

3A quick note on Categories

Categories are just the topic tags (AI, Aerial, Film, Documentary…). Most of the time you’ll simply pick from the existing list.

If you ever need a brand-new one:

1
Click Blog → Categories.
2
Click the + at the top.
3
Type a Title, click Generate for the slug, then Publish.
It’ll now appear as an option whenever you tag posts and videos.

4Good to know

Saving for later
Everything saves automatically as a draft - there’s no “save” button to hunt for. Just close it and return when you like.
Editing something that’s already live
Click the item, make your change, and press Publish again to update it.
Taking something down
Open the item, click the … (more) menu near the top-right, and choose Unpublish. It leaves the site but stays safely in your list.
Green dot vs grey dot
Green = live on the site. Grey = a draft, or changes waiting for you to Publish.
